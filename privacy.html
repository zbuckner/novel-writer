Novel Writer: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Novel Writer is a simple writing app for drafting novels. It runs in your web browser. This page explains what happens to your data. Back to the app

What the app stores

Your manuscript, your notes, and your display settings (font size, theme, and similar) are stored in your browser on your own device. The app has no accounts and no server of its own.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for permission to use the drive.file scope. This lets the app see and manage only the files that the app itself creates in your Google Drive, namely a book file called novel-writer.json and any backup files it makes. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Your book travels directly between your device and your own Google Drive. It does not pass through any server run by the app's author.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, sell, or share your writing, your Google Drive contents, your Google account details, or any analytics. The app has no advertising and no tracking.

How Google user data is used

Data from your Google Drive is used only to save and load your book so it stays in sync across your devices. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files, and may keep ordinary web server logs.
  • Google Fonts supplies the book typeface, so Google may receive your IP address when the font loads.
  • Google Sign-In is used if you connect Google Drive.

Your choices

  • Choose Disconnect in the app's Settings to stop syncing and revoke the app's access.
  • Delete novel-writer.json and any backup files from your Google Drive at any time.
  • Remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clear your browser's site data to erase what is stored on your device.

Contact

Questions can be sent by opening an issue on the GitHub project page that hosts this app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
